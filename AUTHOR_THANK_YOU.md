# Thank you to the paper authors

Thank you to Jie Hu, Lingyun Chen, Geeho Kim, Jinyoung Choi, Bohyung Han,
and Do Young Eun for developing SRMC and making the algorithmic ideas,
assumptions, and experimental targets clear enough to audit.

The constant-memory score-history perspective made a useful clean-room
implementation possible, and the paper's explicit discussion of the relaxed
discrete score helped us report the Static-MNIST mismatch honestly.

This note is an expression of appreciation only; the authors did not review,
endorse, or maintain this independent repository.
